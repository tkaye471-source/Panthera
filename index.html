Stage II — Capital Expansion

Funded payouts are systematically channelled into personal capital. Our target is clear: elevate each trader to a minimum of £20,000 in their own live trading balance.

With capital established, traders transition into elite live accounts with top‑tier brokers. Here, they trade with professional structure, generate consistent monthly returns, and build long‑term personal wealth—not just payouts.
